Privacy Policy for VideoLedger

Last updated: October 10, 2026

VideoLedger helps users organize and track information about videos published across multiple channels and platforms.

Information We Collect

When you connect a third-party platform such as TikTok, VideoLedger may access information authorized by you and permitted by the platform. This may include your basic account information, video metadata, publication details, and performance statistics.

How We Use Information

VideoLedger uses this information to:

  • Display and organize your videos
  • Connect different publications of the same video
  • Show video metadata and performance statistics
  • Provide and improve VideoLedger features

Data Sharing

VideoLedger does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the app or when required by law.

Data Storage and Security

VideoLedger takes reasonable measures to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service. You may disconnect your connected accounts or request deletion of your information by contacting rm7b9h@gmail.com.
